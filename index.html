Double-board PLO where everyone antes, two flops come out, and each player gets one decision: pot or fold. Action starts in the small blind and ends on the button; there is no betting on the turn or river. The bots put money in when their equity against the live field beats the average share, asking a little more in early position with many players still to act, or when the pot odds are good enough against the players already in.

Keys. P pot · F fold · N or Space next hand, at any point: if you haven't acted, you fold and the rest of the hand is played out instantly. Settings apply from the next deal.

Pot odds is the share of the final pot you need to win if nobody behind you comes in. When no one has put money in yet, it's the price against one caller, since if everyone folds you take the antes.

My equity is your share of both boards against random hands, sampled from what you can actually see (your cards and the two flops).

Bots. Standard asks 3% more than the average share for each player still to act, and also pots when its equity against the players already in (plus about a third of those behind) beats the pot odds, with the bar raised 40% per player in, since their hands are better than random. Tight asks 12% more on both tests, Loose 10% less. Classic is the old rule: average share of the live field, nothing else.

EV scores each decision on what you could know when you made it, not on the turn, river or the cards they held. EV loss is what a decision cost against the better choice: $0 when you got it right, however clear the spot. Mistakes adds up the EV losses.

Scoring. Ranges prices potting against the hands the bots actually put in with (their rule, in the spot each one was in) and lets the players behind you come in or fold the same way, averaged over their possible hands; EV is that price when you pot and the ante when you fold. Random hands is the older scoring: every opponent's hand random, the players behind as they really acted (a pot that went to showdown counts at your equity against as many random hands as went in, a pot everyone folded to counts what it won).
