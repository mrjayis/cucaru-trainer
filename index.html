Double-board PLO where everyone antes, two flops come out, and each player gets one decision: put in the pot or fold. Action starts in the small blind and ends on the button; there is no betting on the turn or river. The bots put money in when their equity against the live field beats the average share.
⚖ Open in DBBP calculator
Keys: P pot · F fold · N or Space next hand (at any point: if you haven't acted, you fold and the rest of the hand is played out instantly). Settings apply from the next deal. Pot odds is the share of the final pot you need to win if nobody behind you comes in. When no one has put money in yet, it's the price against one caller, since if everyone folds you take the antes. My equity is your share of both boards against random hands, sampled from what you can actually see (your cards and the two flops).
